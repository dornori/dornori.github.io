🎨 Paint it your way – no limits

When you buy the 3D design files, you're not stuck with a single color palette. You become the art director of your own lamp.

  • Choose any filament color – from glossy neons to stealthy matte blacks. The STL files don't care.
  • Mix transparent & solid parts – create a glowing UFO with translucent PETG for the dome, solid PLA for the base.
  • Try gradient or silk filaments – watch colors shift as the light shines through. No painting required.
  • Swap colors mid‑print (if your printer supports it) – add stripes, rings, or a two‑tone saucer.
  • Future expansion: we're designing color‑change kits and layered “stained glass” effects – but right now, your imagination is the only limit.

💡 Pro tip: Print a small test swatch of each filament with your lamp’s intended infill – light behaves differently through white vs. black vs. transparent.

3

🛠️ Load, tweak, slice – your digital workshop

Got an STL? Great. Now bend it, carve it, add your signature, and turn it into G‑code. All with free, open‑source tools.

✏️ Shape & personalize

  • FreeCAD – load any STL, then round off sharp corners or make them razor‑sharp. Parametric = change your mind later.
  • Blender – sculpt organic curves, add your own text as a raised emblem or deep engraving.
  • Tinkercad – drag, drop, and tweak any model in your browser. Add custom text in seconds.

⚙️ Slice it your way

  • UltiMaker Cura – control rounded corners, fuzzy skin, sharp overhangs.
  • PrusaSlicer – paint supports, add negative volume to carve text.
  • Orca Slicer – built‑in calibration keeps sharp corners sharp.
  • SuperSlicer – corner radius, hole compensation, text embossing.
  • Kiri:Moto – web‑based, add text/logos, slice – no install.

🧰 Refine & enhance

  • MatterControl – design, slice, tweak rounded corners in one place.
  • Meshmixer – smooth edges, stamp text onto any surface.
  • MeshLab – smooth surfaces, fill holes gracefully, enhance crisp corners from scans.

💡 Pro workflow: Load STL → tweak corners & add text → slice → print. All free.

🌍 Tested worldwide – from -5°C to 40°C

Recommended settings for reliable prints across most FDM printers.

  • Material: PLA / PLA+ / PETG / ABS
  • Infill: 20–30% (gyroid or grid)
  • Layer height: 0.2mm standard / 0.15mm quality
  • Supports: None required aside for the UFO Saucer
  • Print time: ~14 hours total for all components

Every structural component is optimised for FDM printing. Only the UFO saucer part requires support.

  • R&D trials were done with several different printers
  • Different materials were tested - PLA, PLA+, ABS
  • Prints were done under extreme conditions to ensure ANYONE has success
  • Tested in temperature range (-5 to 40 degrees celsius)
  • Tested in humid conditions
  • Tested with brittle and moist filament (see video on the right)

💡 Pro tip: Use a brim for tube sections if you need extra adhesion, but geometry is fully support-free.
