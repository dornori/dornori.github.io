1. General Product Information

Q: What is the Dornori Star-A Lamp?

Designed for gardens, patios, beaches, camping, outdoor events, and decorative lighting, it combines elegant 3D-printed aesthetics with reliable, repairable electronics. The lamp's name "Star-A" references its starlight-like illumination pattern and the "A" signifies its premium tier in our product lineup.

Q: What are the main styles available?

The UFO style casts a broader, more dramatic light pattern ideal for events and large gatherings, while the Mushroom offers a cozier, more intimate ambiance perfect for quiet evenings or restaurant patios.   • Maker Edition (3D Print Files): Full parametric and print-ready files for complete customization. Ideal for 3D printing enthusiasts who want to experiment with materials, colors, and modifications.

Q: Who is the Star-A designed for?
A: Garden enthusiasts, campers, makers, event organizers, photographers, interior designers, hospitality businesses, and anyone looking for a unique, portable outdoor lighting solution with a wow factor. It bridges the gap between functional lighting and artistic statement piece.

Q: What makes the Star-A different from other outdoor lamps?

5. Outdoor Use & Durability

Q: Is the Star-A suitable for outdoor use?
A: Yes. It is specifically engineered for outdoor environments with weather-resistant printed parts (using UV-stabilized filament) and protected electronics. The battery compartment is sealed, and all electrical connections are housed in water-resistant enclosures. The lamp performs exceptionally well in gardens, on patios, at campsites, and during outdoor events.

Q: How does it perform in different weather conditions?
A: Suitable for light to moderate rain (IP54 rating). Bring indoors during heavy storms, hail, or prolonged high winds for best longevity. The 3D-printed materials are resilient to temperature fluctuations but extreme heat (above 40°C) or cold (below -5°C) may affect performance. The motor and electronics are designed to handle typical outdoor conditions without degradation.

Q: How should I maintain the lamp for long-term outdoor use?
A: Clean regularly with a soft damp cloth (avoid abrasive materials), check connections periodically (every 3-6 months), and lubricate the kinetic mechanism lightly with silicone-based lubricant if needed. Store indoors during winter months if you live in areas with freezing temperatures. The 3D-printed parts can be gently polished with fine sandpaper if they become scratched or weathered.

Q: How UV-resistant are the printed parts?
A: Using PLA+ or PETG with UV stabilizers, the parts maintain their structural integrity and color for 2-3 years of continuous outdoor exposure. Without UV protection, colors may fade and the material may become brittle. We recommend applying a clear UV-protective spray (available at most hardware stores) for extended outdoor use.

Q: Can the lamp be left outside permanently?
A: While the Star-A is weather-resistant, we recommend bringing it indoors during harsh weather and winter months for maximum longevity. Permanent outdoor installation is possible with additional protective measures such as a cover or sheltered placement. The lamp is designed to be portable, so storing it when not in use is the best practice for long-term durability.

6. Power & Batteries

Q: Which 18650 batteries are recommended?
A: High-quality protected button-top cells from reputable brands such as Samsung (30Q, 35E), LG (HG2, MJ1), Panasonic (NCR18650B), or Sony (VTC6). We recommend cells with at least 3000mAh capacity for optimal runtime. Never use counterfeit or "fire" branded batteries as they pose safety risks. We offer certified battery bundles on our website for your convenience.

Q: How do I safely charge the batteries?
A: Use a dedicated 18650 charger outside the lamp. Never mix old and new batteries or use incompatible chargers. We recommend smart chargers with overcharge protection and balanced charging capabilities. Always charge on a non-flammable surface and never leave charging batteries unattended. Replace batteries if they show signs of damage, swelling, or decreased performance.

Q: Can the lamp be used with solar power?
A: Yes, compatible with 18650 solar chargers and power solutions. Many users combine the Star-A with portable solar panels to create a fully off-grid lighting system. We recommend using a solar charge controller designed for 18650 batteries to ensure safe and efficient charging. Contact our support team for solar-compatible accessory recommendations.

Q: What is the best way to maximize battery life?
A: Use high-capacity cells (3000mAh+), avoid complete discharges (recharge when battery indicator shows 20-30%), store at 40-50% charge when not in use for extended periods, and turn off the lamp when not needed. Avoid exposing batteries to extreme temperatures and always use the same brand and batch of cells for optimal performance. With proper care, high-quality cells can provide 500+ charge cycles.

Q: How do I know when to replace the batteries?
A: Signs that batteries need replacing include significantly reduced runtime, slower rising speed, dimmer light output, or if the batteries become warm during use. We also recommend replacing batteries every 2-3 years for optimal performance, even if they seem to be working fine, as capacity naturally degrades over time.

Q: Can I use rechargeable AA/AAA batteries instead?
A: No. The lamp is specifically designed for 18650 cells which provide the necessary voltage (3.7V each, 11.1V total) and current capacity for the motor and LED. Using lower-voltage batteries will result in poor performance and may damage the electronics.

10. Troubleshooting Common Issues

Q: The lamp does not power on or rise.
A:

  • Verify battery polarity (proper orientation of all 3 batteries)
  • Check battery charge level (recharge if needed)
  • Inspect all Wago connections (ensure wires are fully inserted)
  • Confirm lever switch is in the correct position
  • Check that the LED module is properly connected
  • Test batteries individually with a multimeter (should read 3.7-4.2V)
If all checks fail, contact support for advanced diagnostics.

Q: The LED light is dim or flickers.
A:

  • Check battery voltage (recharge if below 3.5V per cell)
  • Verify LED connections (Wago connectors may be loose)
  • Replace batteries if they are old or damaged
  • Check for corrosion on battery contacts
  • Test with fresh batteries to isolate the issue
Persistent flickering may indicate a faulty LED driver.

Q: Parts do not fit perfectly during assembly.
A: Minor variations due to filament tolerances, print settings, or material shrinkage are normal. Try:

  • Light sanding with fine-grit sandpaper (400-600 grit)
  • Gentle pressure during assembly (parts are designed to create a snug fit)
  • Warming parts slightly (e.g., with a hair dryer) to make them more flexible
  • Checking if parts are oriented correctly (some have specific alignment requirements)
If parts are significantly oversized, reprint with adjusted settings or contact us for replacement.

Q: The motor makes noise or struggles.
A:

  • Check for obstructions in the mechanism (dust, debris, or loose parts)
  • Ensure proper lubrication (apply a small amount of silicone-based lubricant to the mechanism)
  • Verify battery voltage (low batteries cause the motor to struggle)
  • Check that the mechanism is properly aligned
Persistent grinding or unusual noises may indicate a gear issue — contact support for a motor replacement.

Q: The lamp rises unevenly or wobbles.
A:

  • Ensure the base is on a flat, level surface
  • Check that all sections are properly seated and aligned
  • Verify that the mechanism is clean and free of debris
  • Tighten any loose hardware (screws, nuts)
For persistent wobbling, check if the printed parts have warped — reprinting affected parts may be necessary.

16. Battery & Power Management (Detailed)

Q: What happens if I use non-protected batteries?
A: We strongly recommend protected cells for safety. Non-protected batteries may pose risks of over-discharge, over-current, or short circuit. Protected cells include a built-in protection circuit that prevents these issues. Using unprotected cells voids your warranty and can damage the lamp or create a fire hazard.

Q: How long do the batteries typically last before needing replacement?
A: With proper care, high-quality 18650 cells can provide hundreds of charge cycles (300-500 cycles). This translates to 3-5 years of regular use depending on usage frequency. Signs that batteries need replacing include significantly reduced runtime, slower lifting speed, or physical swelling of the cells.

Q: Can I use batteries with different capacities?
A: No. All three batteries should be identical (same brand, model, capacity, and age). Mixing batteries with different capacities or ages can cause imbalances, reduced performance, and potential safety issues. Always buy batteries as a matched set and replace all three together.

Q: Does the lamp have over-discharge protection?
A: The lamp's electronics include a low-voltage cutoff that disconnects the load when battery voltage drops below a safe threshold. However, this should not replace the protection circuit in high-quality protected cells, and we recommend using protected cells as the primary safety measure.

22. Advanced Troubleshooting

Q: What if the lever mechanism sticks?
A: Check alignment of the lever and contact point. Lightly lubricate the moving parts with silicone-based lubricant. Make sure there is no debris or obstruction. If the lever switch is damaged, replacement switches are available in our spare parts store. For accurate alignment, refer to the assembly manual for orientation guidance.

Q: The lamp rises but the light doesn't turn on.
A:

  • Check the LED module connection to the driver
  • Verify that the switch is in the "on" position
  • Test with a multimeter if voltage reaches the LED
  • Check for damaged wiring between the driver and LED
If the LED is faulty, contact us for a replacement module.

Q: The motor stops mid-extension.
A:

  • Check battery voltage (may be low)
  • Check for mechanical obstruction
  • Inspect the motor connections
  • Ensure the lever switch is fully engaged
  • Allow the motor to cool if it's been running continuously
If the motor repeatedly stops, it may be faulty and need replacement.

Q: How do I reset the lamp?
A: Remove batteries, wait 30 seconds, and reinstall. This resets the electronics and clears any temporary faults. If the issue persists, check all connections and charge the batteries fully. For smart versions (future), a reset button will be included.
