Our Story

Born on an Ancient Celtic hill,
Raised at Will

Dornori started with a simple problem: a group of friends on a Celtic hill an open clearing with no light that felt right. Star‑A grew from that moment — a portable, beautiful lamp you can buy ready‑made or build yourself.

Where it started

Dornori was born at a forest gathering. A group of friends sitting in a clearing — music playing, food spread across a blanket, the sky going dark — no trees around to hang a torch from. Phones came out.White light on faces, everyone looking like they were telling ghost stories. What we wanted was a warm light from above. Something made for moments like this.

That one moment became the Star‑A: a portable outdoor lamp that rises on command, runs on batteries, and looks like nothing you've seen before. A pole extends from a compact base — carrying a glowing UFO or Mushroom lamp head up to 190 cm — driven by a DC motor, controlled by a simple lever switch.

No fire. No power outlet. No disposable gimmick. Ready to take with you, or something you can build yourself, there's 3 different option for different people.

A unique one of a kind product that is a must have for people that love the outdoors or that like to make things their own.

What makes it different

  • ✅ It moves — the pole rises, the lamp climbs. Nothing else does this.
  • ✅ Real components — DC motor, Faston connectors, Wago lever terminals. Industry-standard parts you can source anywhere.
  • ✅ Off-the-shelf parts — nothing proprietary. If something breaks, replace it yourself in minutes.
  • ✅ Outdoors by design — 3× protected 18650 batteries, compact base, genuinely portable. No extension cord needed.
  • ✅ Two distinct models — UFO and Mushroom. Four colourways each. Pick one or build both.
  • ✅ Open source — PY, STL, OBJ, and 3MF files for every printed part. Print your own or order ours.
  • ✅ Repairable forever — every part available separately. No planned obsolescence, no subscriptions, no cloud.

The Star‑A is designed to last — not because it's sealed shut and impossible to touch, but because you can fix it, upgrade it, and pass it on. That's the whole point.

Who builds (and uses) a Star‑A

Designed for builders aged 8–12 working with an adult. In practice, it finds its way to a lot of different people:

  • 🧒 Kids and teens — who want a light that goes with them and looks genuinely cool
  • 👨‍👧 Families — who want a weekend project that produces something worth using
  • 👴 Grandparents — who want to build something memorable with grandchildren
  • 🏫 Classrooms & STEM — consistent, documentable, genuinely engaging
  • 🖨️ Makers with 3D printers — print, source, wire, build, take outside

"There's something that happens the first time a child flips the lever and watches the pole rise. Their eyes go wide. Then they say: Wait — I made that? That's the moment. That's what Dornori is for."
