The DaVinci Prototyping Collection

Born from 3D printing prototypes and inspired by Leonardo's timeless sketches to lower our footprint and elevate your space.

Art from Innovation

Every masterpiece starts with trial and error. During the development of our Kickstarter campaign for the Star-A mushroom lamp — our magical telescopic lamp powered by a protruding 1.85m iron rod — we generated experimental 3D printed parts.

Instead of letting them go to waste, we transformed our prototyping prints into a unique, limited-edition art series based on Leonardo da Vinci's classic sketches.
