Sustainable Art Series

The DaVinci Prototyping Collection

Born from 3D printing prototypes and inspired by Leonardo's timeless sketches to lower our footprint and elevate your space.

Art from Innovation & Glorious Failure

Every masterpiece starts with trial, error, and an overwhelming amount of filament. During the development of our Kickstarter campaign for the Star-A mushroom lamp — our magical telescopic lamp powered by a protruding 1.85m iron rod — we generated some... *wildly creative* experimental 3D printed parts.

Instead of letting them go to waste, we immortalized our most hilarious prototyping screw-ups into a unique, limited-edition art series based on Leonardo da Vinci's classic sketches. Own a piece of our R&D department's soul!
